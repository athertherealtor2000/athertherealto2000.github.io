About Us

Our ornamental fish journey began in Dubai, inspired by a passion for aquatic life and the beauty of the aquarium hobby. We are now expanding our journey to Pakistan, bringing that same enthusiasm to a growing community of fishkeepers and aquascaping enthusiasts.

We specialise in ornamental freshwater shrimp, aquatic plants and aquascaping, and we also deal in a wide variety of ornamental fish. Whether you are building your first aquarium, creating a planted aquascape or looking for beautiful fish and shrimp, Aqua Man is here to share the journey with you.
