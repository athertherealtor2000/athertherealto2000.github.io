Variety profile

Cherry Red • Neocaridina davidi

The classic red Neocaridina shrimp and one of the most popular beginner-friendly ornamental shrimp.

Adult sizeAdult females about 2.5–3 cm; males about 2–2.5 cm.
ColourLight red through solid cherry red, depending on line and grade.
TemperamentPeaceful; best in a shrimp-focused community
DifficultyBeginner-friendly in stable, mature water

Colour & grade selection

Common hobby grades include lower-grade pale/translucent red through stronger, more opaque red selections. Naming varies by seller.

A hardy, prolific shrimp when kept in a mature, stable freshwater aquarium.

Habitat & aquarium setup

In nature, Neocaridina davidi are associated with freshwater streams and vegetated habitats. In the aquarium, provide a mature, planted tank with moss, fine-leaved plants, driftwood and surfaces that grow biofilm. Use a sponge filter or a guarded intake so shrimplets cannot be pulled into the filter. Provide gentle flow and plenty of grazing area.

Temperature20–25°C recommended target
pHAbout 6.5–8.0
GHAbout 6–8 dGH
KHAbout 2–6 dKH
TDSOften 150–250 ppm; interpret with GH/KH
Tank maturityFully cycled and biologically stable

These are practical general husbandry ranges, not rigid requirements. Acclimate gradually and prioritise stable water that matches the breeder's conditions.

Daily care & feeding

  • Allow natural biofilm and algae to develop on safe surfaces.
  • Offer a varied shrimp-specific food in small portions; remove uneaten food.
  • Use botanicals and moss as grazing surfaces, ensuring leaves are aquarium-safe.
  • Perform modest, consistent water changes with temperature- and parameter-matched water.
  • Avoid copper-containing medications, pesticides and sudden parameter swings.
  • Keep with peaceful, small tankmates only; fish may eat shrimplets.

Size, breeding & community notes

Females usually grow larger and appear broader-bodied than males. Mature females may carry eggs beneath the abdomen. Under suitable conditions, females release miniature shrimplets rather than free-swimming larvae. The colour line breeds true best when kept separate from other Neocaridina colour strains; mixed lines can produce variable or wild-type offspring over generations.

For a breeding colony, use a dedicated shrimp aquarium. For a community display, select only peaceful tankmates and provide dense cover.
