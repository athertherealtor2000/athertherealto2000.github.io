Neocaridina Care & Breeding

Neocaridina shrimp are popular freshwater aquarium invertebrates. With stable water parameters, suitable nutrition, filtration and a mature aquarium, they can reproduce successfully in captivity. Maintaining separate breeding lines helps preserve individual colour varieties.
