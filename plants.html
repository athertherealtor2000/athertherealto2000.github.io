Christmas moss and Java moss for attaching to wood and stone, creating texture and shelter for shrimp.

Floating Plants

Phyllanthus fluitans (Red Root Floater) and other floating plants for natural cover and nutrient uptake.

Rotala rotundifolia, Limnophila sessiliflora and other attractive stems for planted aquariums.
